Who We Are

Lagos' Premier Luxury Beverage Supplier

Drinks Store by MrKay was founded on one principle: that anyone in Lagos — whether a private individual, a boutique lounge, or a Fortune 500 company planning a corporate event — deserves access to authentic, premium-grade spirits and beverages delivered with complete professionalism.

Based in Agungi, Ajiran, we have spent years building relationships with authorised global distributors and importers to ensure every bottle we supply is 100% authentic. In a market filled with counterfeits and inflated premiums, we are the name Lagos trusts.

We are now positioned for international growth — seeking distribution partnerships with global beverage brands looking to access the fast-growing Nigerian luxury market.
